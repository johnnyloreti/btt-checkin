What is new

Same page, same PIN. There are four new tabs along the top and more on each member's screen. Nothing about checking in has changed.

Stripes CHANGED

Under the kids who are ready to be looked at, a second list: One class away. Check it in the morning and you know who would cross the line at tonight's class.

  • No button on those rows. Their next check-in moves them up.
  • The count is a prompt, not a promise. The coach decides.

Belts NEW

Rank shows beside every name on a class roster and on the member's screen. Everyone starts as a white belt until you say otherwise.

  • Record a belt: open the member, pick the belt and the stripes on it, tap twice.
  • Tick They already hold this rank for someone who walked in with it. That keeps their class count.
  • Give a stripe now works for anyone, any time.
  • Wrong tap? Undo removes the last one.

Away NEW TAB

Who has gone quiet. Active members whose last class was 14 or more days ago, longest first, with the date. Tap a name to open them.

  • Below it: members on the roster who have never checked in.

Trends NEW TAB

Check-ins for every class on the schedule, week by week, for the last four weeks. This week is on the right.

  • A dot means the class has not happened yet this week. A zero means nobody came.
  • The bottom rows count all check-ins and how many different people trained.

Plan and lesson notes NEW TAB

Write what a class will cover. Two levels: a plan for the week for each program, and a note for one class.

  • On Tonight, each class shows the first line of its lesson.
  • Open a class to add or edit either one, above the names.
  • The Plan tab lays out a whole week. Use the arrows to plan ahead.
  • Save it blank to remove it.

Progress page NEW

Every member has a page for their phone: class count, belt and stripes, recent classes. A parent's page shows all of their kids.

  • On the member's screen, Text it sends the link to the phone on file, the parent's for a kid. Tap twice.
  • Copy link if you would rather send it yourself.
  • It shows nothing about money, and no stripe countdown.

What members will ask you

How do I see my progress?Scan the poster, or go to checkin.bttbridgewater.com/member. Type your name, then your kiosk PIN, or tap Text me my link.

I do not have a PIN, or I forgot it.On that page, tap Set up or reset my PIN. A link comes by text. Or open their member screen here and tap Set up PIN while they are at the desk.

It says my PIN is easy to guess.New PINs cannot be one digit four times or four in a row, like 1111 or 1234. Any other four digits are fine. PINs people already have still work.

It says PIN login is paused.Too many wrong tries on that page today. It clears on its own within a day. The text link still works, and so does the kiosk.

My kid's page is missing.The kid is not linked to that parent yet. Tell Johnny which parent, and he will link them.

Drinks:the kiosk now offers Water $1, Gatorade $2, Hydrate $3 and Protein $4.

Family: a member's screen now lists their siblings and a parent who trains here, with rank, waiver and last class. Tap one to open them.
The iPad at the desk now picks up updates on its own when nobody is using it. If something on the staff page looks old, reload the page.
